Straight answers

Questions? Asked & answered.

Objections handled before you call. Still curious? Ask me anything.

Are you licensed?

I'm IBEW-trained — that's the union electrician program, the gold standard for electrical training. I'm working toward my state license now. What I can promise: I do the work right, I give you a written quote first, and I stand behind every job. Call (228) 224-9150 and ask me anything.

How much does it cost?

Depends on the job — but I'll always give you a written, itemized quote before any work starts. No surprises, no "while I'm here" upsells. For most jobs, I give you three options (good / better / best) so you pick what fits your budget. Call for a free estimate. It costs nothing to ask.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Free estimates, free assessments. I'll come look, tell you what's going on, and give you a written quote. If you don't hire me, you still got a professional opinion for free. What have you got to lose? Call (228) 224-9150.

How fast can you get here?

For emergencies — sparking, burning smell, power out — I move fast. For planned work, I usually book within a few days. Call and I'll give you a straight answer on timing. Emergencies: call now. Everything else: call and let's schedule.

Will you show up on time?

Yes. And if something changes, I'll call you — not the other way around. Showing up when I say I will is the easiest thing in this business, and somehow half the trade can't manage it. Try me. (228) 224-9150.

Do you guarantee your work?

I stand behind every job. If something I installed fails because of my workmanship, I'll make it right. That's not a corporate warranty with fine print — that's my name on the business. Call and let's talk about your project.

What areas do you serve?

Biloxi, Gulfport, Ocean Springs, D'Iberville, Long Beach, and the surrounding Mississippi Gulf Coast. If you're close and I've got the truck loaded, I'll come to you. Not sure if I cover you? Call and ask.

Can I just text you a picture of the problem?

Absolutely. Sometimes I can tell you over the phone whether it's urgent or can wait — saves us both time. Call (228) 224-9150 and we'll figure it out.
