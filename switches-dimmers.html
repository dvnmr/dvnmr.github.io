Switches are the most-touched thing in your house — and the cheapest upgrade with the biggest feel. Dim the dining room. Put the porch on a timer. Charge your phone without a brick. Small jobs, done right, that change how the house feels every single day.

What I install

  • Standard switch replacement — worn, cracked, or builder-grade beige, gone
  • Dimmer switches — dining rooms, bedrooms, anywhere mood matters
  • Smart switches — phone & voice control, schedules, 3-way smart setups
  • USB combo outlets — USB-A + USB-C built right into the outlet
  • GFCI outlet installation & replacement — kitchens, baths, outdoors, garage
  • Outlet upgrades — tamper-resistant, 20A, isolated ground where it counts
  • Whole-room switch & outlet refreshes — one visit, every device new

The details matter

Back-stabbed outlets are the #1 cause of dead outlets I get called for. I side-wire or pigtail every device — the connection that doesn't loosen up in five years. And every dimmer gets matched to the actual bulbs in the room, so there's no flicker.
