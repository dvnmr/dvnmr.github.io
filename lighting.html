Good lighting changes everything — how the kitchen feels at 7pm, whether the backyard is usable after dark, whether your house looks alive from the street. I install it all: the fixtures you picked out, the ones you haven't thought of yet, and the wiring that makes them work.

Indoor lighting

  • Light fixture installation & replacement — chandeliers, pendants, flush mounts
  • Recessed/can lighting — new installs and retrofits
  • Under-cabinet lighting — kitchens that actually show the countertops
  • Accent & display lighting — shelves, art, architectural features
  • Closet, pantry & laundry lighting — the rooms everyone forgets

Outdoor lighting

  • Landscape lighting — path, accent, and tree lighting that holds up in salt air
  • Security & flood lighting — motion-activated, properly aimed
  • Porch, patio & soffit lighting — outdoor living after dark
  • Post & bollard lights — driveway and walkway

Outdoor gear on the coast takes a beating — salt air, humidity, storm season. I use marine-rated fixtures, sealed connections, and corrosion-resistant hardware — the stuff built for waterfront, not the indoor gear in a weatherproof box that the other guys install. On this coast, that's the difference between lighting that lasts 15 years and lighting you replace every 3.
