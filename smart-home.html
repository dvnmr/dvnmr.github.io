Smart home gear is only smart if it's wired right and set up right. A switch with no neutral, a doorbell on a dying transformer, a camera with no signal — that's where most DIY installs go sideways. I handle the electrical side and get everything talking to your phone before I leave.

What I install

  • Smart switches & dimmers — single-pole, 3-way, no-neutral solutions
  • Smart thermostats — Nest, Ecobee, Honeywell (C-wire added if you need one)
  • Video doorbells — Ring, Google, wired or battery with proper chime setup
  • Smart lighting & automated scenes — rooms that set themselves
  • Home security cameras — wired power where it counts, no dead batteries
  • Smart locks — keypad and phone-controlled entry
  • Whole-home WiFi help — outlet and wiring placement for mesh systems
