Biloxi homes, Biloxi wiring

Biloxi is a mix: 1960s ranch homes north of the interstate with original panels, Back Bay cottages that have survived every storm since Camille, new construction booming out in Woolmarket and Cedar Lake, and rentals all along the beach corridor. Every era has its own wiring quirks.

The older homes? Watch for undersized 100-amp services, aluminum branch wiring, and panels the salt air has been eating for thirty years. The new builds? Usually solid, but the builder-grade devices fail early and the surge protection nobody installed becomes real obvious after the first summer thunderstorm. We know both — and everything in between.

Close to the water, everything electrical works harder: corrosion on terminals, GFCI devices that nuisance-trip in the humidity, exterior fixtures that give up after two seasons. We install with marine-grade thinking because on this coast, that's just good practice.
