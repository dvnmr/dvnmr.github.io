Common questions

Why do many smart switches fail in older Gulf Coast homes?+
Most Wi-Fi and Zigbee smart switches require a true neutral wire inside the switch box. Older homes often have switch loops with no neutral — we run or reconfigure clean neutral paths where needed.
Do you install smart thermostats like Nest and Ecobee cleanly?+
Yes. If your existing thermostat cable lacks a dedicated C-wire, we properly connect a C-wire adapter or pull new 18/5–18/8 thermostat cable so the unit doesn't pulse-steal HVAC relay power.
Can you wire video doorbells, floodlight cameras, and smart EV chargers?+
Yes — dedicated transformers for doorbells, weatherproof exterior junction boxes for Ring/Nest floodlight cams, and Wi-Fi Level 2 EV wall connectors.
Do I buy the smart devices or can you advise before ordering?+
Call (228) 224-9150 before you buy boxes full of dimmers — we check whether your switches are 3-way, missing neutrals, or paired with dimmable LED drivers first.
