  • Smart fan controls — remote, wall control, or phone/app control

Why the box matters

A ceiling fan is 30+ pounds of spinning weight. Hung from a regular light box, it's a matter of time. I check every mount — and if the box isn't fan-rated, I put one in. That's the difference between a fan that lasts 20 years and one that ends up on the floor.
