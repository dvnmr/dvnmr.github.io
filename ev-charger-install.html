Common questions

Should I hardwire my Level 2 EV charger or install a NEMA 14-50 outlet?+
Hardwiring allows up to 48A continuous charging (on a 60A circuit) and removes an extra plug connection that can heat up under daily loads. A commercial/EV-rated NEMA 14-50 outlet works well up to 40A continuous if you plan to unplug and take the unit when moving.
Can my existing 100-amp panel handle a home EV charger?+
Sometimes yes depending on whether you run gas vs electric appliances, or via an EV load-management controller — otherwise a 200A upgrade is the safest fix. Run our free Panel Checker tool first, then we check exact amp draw on site.
Do I buy the EV charger before calling?+
Either way works. Tell me what vehicle you drive and whether your wall connector is Tesla Wall Connector, ChargePoint, Grizzl-E, or OEM — I supply the right wire gauge, conduit, breaker, and EV-rated receptacle if needed.
How fast does an average home EV charger install happen?+
If the panel is in the attached garage, most circuits take a few hours to complete, torque, and test under full load so you plug in that same night.
