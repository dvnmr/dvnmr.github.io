I use marine-rated fixtures, sealed connections, and corrosion-resistant hardware — the stuff built for waterfront, not the indoor gear in a weatherproof box that the other guys install. Common questions

Do I need attic access to install recessed canless LED wafer lights?+
Not always. Low-profile canless wafer LEDs slip right under most ceiling drywall thicknesses even around joists, though running new switch legs still benefits from clean routing between bays.
What color temperature works best indoors vs outdoors on the Coast?+
2700K–3000K warm white keeps living rooms, dining rooms, and coastal porches cozy without harsh glare, while 4000K neutral white works nicely in garages, closets, and utility rooms.
Can you wire waterfront dock lighting, soffit downlights, and security floods?+
Yes — saltwater-resistant outdoor fixtures, sealed boxes, dusk-to-dawn photocells, and clean conduit runs built for coastal wind and spray.
Do you hang heavy chandeliers or high foyer fixtures?+
Yes — including proper structural box support, scaffolding/tall ladder work, dimmer pairing, and clean centering over dining tables or stairwells.
