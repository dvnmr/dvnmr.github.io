Electrical panel upgrades

YOUR PANEL IS THE HEART.

⚡ Call (228) 224-9150 Get an Estimate

If your panel is more than 25 years old, has breakers that trip for no reason, or you're adding anything that draws real power — EV charger, hot tub, workshop — your panel is probably overdue.

Old, undersized, or Federal Pacific / Zinsco panels are fire risks. I replace them with modern, safe service — done right.

Signs you need an upgrade

  • Breakers trip constantly or won't reset
  • Lights dim when the AC kicks on
  • Panel feels warm or smells like burning
  • You're out of breaker slots
  • Federal Pacific, Zinsco, or Challenger panel (known fire risks)
  • Adding an EV charger, generator, or major appliance
