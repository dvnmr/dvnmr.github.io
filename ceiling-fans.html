Why the box matters

A ceiling fan is 30+ pounds of spinning weight. Hung from a regular light box, it's a matter of time. I check every mount — and if the box isn't fan-rated, I put one in. That's the difference between a fan that lasts 20 years and one that ends up on the floor.

Common questions

Can any light fixture box support a heavy ceiling fan?+
No. Standard plastic light boxes crack and sag under fan weight and torque. Whenever we convert a light to a fan, we install a properly braced UL-listed fan-rated box anchored to framing.
Can you install ceiling fans on tall or vaulted Gulf Coast ceilings?+
Yes — including proper downrod sizing, sloped-ceiling canopies, and safe access without wobbling or unbalanced blades.
Do you install outdoor damp-rated or wet-rated fans for covered porches?+
Yes — screened patios, elevated Coastal porches, and outdoor kitchens require damp/wet-rated hardware and sealed box connections so humidity doesn't ruin bearings or remotes.
How can I get separate wall switches for fan speed and light?+
If your ceiling only has a single switch leg today, we can fish dual switch conductors or cleanly configure wall/remote controls as quoted up front.
