Common questions

How do you price emergency electrical calls?+
A visit and assessment is $75, stated before I drive out and credited toward the job if you hire me. Same-day or after-hours emergency premiums are disclosed up front, and you get a written quote before any repair begins.
What counts as an immediate electrical emergency?+
A burning plastic smell near an outlet or panel, buzzing or crackling inside the breaker box, visible arcing, scorch marks, warm breaker covers, or partial power loss after a storm — shut off affected breakers if safe and call (228) 224-9150 immediately.
Should I keep resetting a breaker that trips twice?+
No. A breaker that trips again right away is actively protecting your wiring from an overload or short circuit. Repeatedly forcing it back on overheats contacts and wiring.
Can I text a photo before you drive out?+
Yes. Call (228) 224-9150 or text a picture of a tripped panel, scorched receptacle, or storm damage so I can tell you what to turn off safely while I head over.
